# Method: seed germination

**What is measured.** SAM finds "mung bean" and "root" in each jar. Argus reports the total length of the roots in each jar in millimetres, sized by the Argus Card. A jar reads zero until SAM first finds a root; after that, finding no root is a miss (a gap with a "missed" event). A picture SAM couldn't outline is a gap too.

**Typical curve.** A quiet start while the beans soak and swell, then roots appearing one by one, then steady growth that may level off. Sprouting is uneven: a jar's total length mixes how many beans sprouted with how long their roots are.

**What counts as a real change.** Growth above the normal wobble between check-ins, seen in more than one jar of the same condition. Compare test jars with control jars at the same time, never a jar with its own earlier self alone.

**Other possible causes and how to check them.**
- Temperature: jars nearer a heat source or window grow faster.
- Uneven watering: a drier jar slows down; look for handling events and for one jar lagging alone.
- Position in the row: light and warmth differ along the row; conditions should alternate, not sit in blocks.
- Seed batch: beans from one packet vary; repeat jars per condition matter more than any single jar.
- Day and night: in a dark room pictures may be skipped; a gap at night is not a stop in growth.
