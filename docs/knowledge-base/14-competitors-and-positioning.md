# 14 · Competitors and positioning

> Researched mid-October 2026. Re-check before quoting in the pitch.

| Product / project | What it is | How Argus differs |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Lumi (LabEye camera module)** by Reach Industries (UK) | Enterprise lab platform using computer vision, voice and ML to capture and archive lab data (color, phase, overflows, instrument readings). Presented at Chemical UK Expo, May 2026. Sold to companies via sales/demos; no public price | Argus is for everyone else (students, teachers, small teams, hobbyists); reasons against a hypothesis, gives verdicts, highlights reel, smart trigger, next-run loop, plant control |
| **Pion LabEye** (Pion Inc.) | Separate, unrelated multi-camera lab video recorder (Windows app); records only, no AI reasoning | Name confusion only |
| **AC Infinity Spectron** grow cameras | Plant cameras that sync with AI controllers (e.g. dim lights on heat stress); grow tents only; cheaper model ~$79.99 | Argus is general purpose; plants are one recipe |
| **PlantCam** | Timelapse camera, no AI analysis | Argus measures and reasons |
| **Self-driving labs** (Toronto's Ada, Argonne) | Full closed loop with robots; very expensive and engineering-heavy | Argus brings the loop to a desk with a webcam |
| **LabOS** (research) | Smart glasses + specially trained vision-language model for procedure error detection in wet labs | Argus needs no custom training (SAM 3.1 zero-shot) and watches the experiment, not the person |
| **ReShape Biotech** imaging (case study) | Automated plate imaging; one lab said events "could take days or weeks to appear, or occur overnight when no one was in the lab" | Evidence of demand |
| 2026 study on camera AI in labs | Scientists imagined camera AI as an "auxiliary monitor" providing attentional scaffolding | Evidence of demand |

## Positioning statement

"Self-driving-lab intelligence for everyone who can't afford a self-driving lab." Competitors existing helps: they prove labs will pay for camera-based monitoring. Judges reward strong working execution more than being first.

## Name note

"Argus" is a common name in tech. Before publishing widely, search "Argus AI lab" to avoid a direct clash (team name: Argus Labs).
